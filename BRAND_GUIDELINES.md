# Riverside Garden Villa — Brand Guidelines

## Positioning

Riverside Garden Villa is a private riverside villa in Puncak for families and close groups who want a cozy, refreshing stay surrounded by garden, stone, wood, and cool mountain air.

## Brand Feel

Warm, natural, calm, private, refined-but-approachable.

Avoid: flashy luxury, hotel-formal language, overly rustic/old vibes, cheap homestay energy, loud Instagram gimmicks.

## Core Tagline

Your private riverside escape in Puncak.

## Voice

Warm, simple, grounded, welcoming.

Good example:

> Wake up to the sound of the river, gather around the long dining table, and enjoy cool Puncak air with the people you came with.

Avoid:

> Indulge in an exquisite premium nature retreat experience.

## Audience

- Families
- Close groups of friends
- Guests looking for a private Puncak getaway
- People who want nature, comfort, and shared spaces without overly formal luxury

## Key Selling Points

- Riverside setting
- Private garden
- 3 bedrooms
- Cool Puncak air
- Very private
- Service help available
- Big dining table and patio
- Cozy living room
- Kitchen, fridge, WiFi

## Typography

Current implementation:

- Headings: `Cormorant Garamond` — heritage villa serif, aligned with the logo mood.
- Body/UI: `Manrope` — clean modern sans, warm and readable.

Typography direction:

- Use elegant serif for emotional/brand moments.
- Use clean sans for body, buttons, navigation, and practical information.
- Use uppercase tracking for small labels and CTAs.

## Photo Style

- Natural light, warm but not orange.
- Greens should look lush, not neon.
- Preserve stone/wood texture.
- Avoid harsh HDR.
- Avoid fake luxury staging.
- Listing edits should remove distractions but not make the villa feel unreal.

## Website Structure

1. Hero — brand proposition + booking CTA.
2. Quick highlights — 3BR, riverside garden, private villa, cool air, kitchen, WiFi, service help.
3. Villa story — warm narrative intro.
4. Living & dining — group gathering spaces.
5. Bedrooms — calm rooms after outdoor time.
6. Gallery — exterior, bedrooms, living, riverside garden, kitchen.
7. Riverside SEO section — targets Puncak/private/riverside garden villa searches.
8. Amenities.
9. FAQ.
10. Final booking CTA.
